title: "Saving Commands"
published: "2026-10-01T00:00:00Z"
---

`CTRL + u` will delete + save everything from the cursor to the beginning of the line

`CTRL + k` will do the same thing but from cursor to the end of the line

Use `CTRL + y` to then paste + execute it

---

Usage:

Tyep command.

Remember you need to do something else. `CTRL + u`

Do something else.

`CTRL + y`
